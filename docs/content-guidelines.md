# Penny Pal content guidelines

A living guide for all user-facing copy. Tanya (content designer) owns it and
adds to it. Follow it on every screen and message. Add new rules as they come up.

## Voice and style
- **Use contractions.** Write "it's", "that's", "you're", "don't", not "it is",
  "that is", "you are", "do not". It reads warmer and more human.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, colons, parentheses, or separate sentences.
- **Plain language, no jargon.** If a term might be unfamiliar, use a simpler word.

## Tone
- **Show, don't tell.** Don't use mood adjectives like "calm", "gentle",
  "relaxed", or "soothing" in the interface. The anti-anxiety feeling comes from
  clear numbers, reassuring facts, honest framing, the visuals, and the absence
  of shaming, not from announcing a mood.
- **No shaming.** Never scold. Frame limits as suggestions the user can change.

## Substance
- **Cite a credible source** when the app makes a recommendation (for example,
  the 50/30/20 rule from Elizabeth Warren, All Your Worth). A named source builds
  trust, which matters most for money.
